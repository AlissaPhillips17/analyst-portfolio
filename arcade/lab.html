I'm Alissa. I work across digital analytics, experimentation, and marketing technology. I investigate measurement issues, help teams launch experiments, and turn stakeholder requests into requirements people can build and test.

At Universal Destinations & Experiences, my work includes personalization and experimentation across the Orlando and Hollywood ecommerce properties: test strategy, Adobe Target setup, backlog prioritization, and QA before launch.

My background also includes transactional validation and analytics troubleshooting at Chipotle; implementation across Peacock, Fandango, Fandango at Home, and Rotten Tomatoes at NBCUniversal; and SQL analysis, dashboards, and restaurant performance reporting at Subway.

I like getting specific: what are we trying to learn, what does this metric actually count, and what needs to work before we ship? That is the thinking behind the projects here.
